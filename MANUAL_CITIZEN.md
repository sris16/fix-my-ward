# 👤 Citizen Portal User Manual

## 1. Registration & Login
- Navigate to `http://localhost:5173/register` to create a citizen account.
- Sign in at `http://localhost:5173/login`.

---

## 2. Reporting a Civic Issue (`/report`)
1. Click **Report Issue**.
2. Enter issue title, description, category, and location text.
3. Select exact map coordinates on the interactive location map.
4. Upload photos (optional) and submit.

---

## 3. Tracking Reports (`/my-reports`)
- Track status updates on reported complaints (`Pending` -> `Verified` -> `Assigned` -> `In Progress` -> `Resolved`).

---

## 4. Public Reports & Community Upvoting (`/public-reports`)
- Browse complaints submitted across your municipality and upvote issues in your neighborhood to increase priority.

---

## 5. Trust Levels & Badges (`/profile`)
- Earn contribution scores and civic badges as your reported issues get resolved by municipal teams.
